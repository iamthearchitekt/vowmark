# VOWMARK Font Intelligence System

VOWMARK curates licensed typefaces according to detailed graphic classifications:

## Font Classifications & Suitability
- **Didone Serif** (Bodoni Moda): French Editorial & high-fashion black tie.
- **High-Contrast Old Style Serif** (Cormorant Garamond): Editorial Luxury couple wordmarks.
- **Classical Roman Serif** (Cinzel): European Estate heraldic monograms.
- **Transitional Serif** (Playfair Display): Traditional wedding invitations.
- **Minimal Sans** (Inter): Clean modern minimalist logos and dates.
- **Formal Calligraphy** (Alex Brush): Romantic script accents only.
